Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I arrive for service?
We recommend arriving 15-20 minutes before the service starts to find good parking, check your children into their departments, and find a comfortable seat.

Is there a ministry for children?
Yes! We have a vibrant Children's Ministry where kids are taught the Word of God in a fun, age-appropriate environment. We also have a Teens Church.

How can I become a member?
You can join our Membership Class (Foundation School) which runs periodically. Please visit the information desk after service or contact us via the form above for the next schedule.
