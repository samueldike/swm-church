Our Origin & Beliefs

Every Dead Dream
Can Live Again

{{ siteName }} was born out of a divine mandate to restore hope to the hopeless. We believe that no situation is too far gone for God's redeeming power. Our ministry is anchored on the scripture Job 14:7-9, which declares that at the scent of water, a tree that was cut down will bud and bring forth boughs like a plant.

We emphasize the transformative power of God's grace over religion. We are not just a church; we are a movement dedicated to showing men and women that their dead dreams, lost opportunities, and broken lives can be revived through the power of the Holy Spirit.

Founder & Senior Pastor

Samuel Inyeneabasi

Leadership

A Mandate of
Restoration

Pastor Samuel Inyeneabasi is a dynamic teacher of God's word, a revivalist, and a visionary leader. He carries a specific mandate to reposition men for God's very best through the teaching of the Word of Faith and the demonstration of the Spirit and Power.

His ministry is characterized by a unique grace to simplify complex spiritual truths, making them applicable to daily life. He is a mentor to many, a loving husband, and a father.
