“For there is hope for a tree, if it is cut down, that it will sprout again, and that its tender shoots will not cease. Though its root may grow old in the earth, and its stump may die in the ground, yet at the scent of water it will bud and bring forth branches like a plant.”

{# ═══ III · WELCOME HOME ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════ #}

You belong here

Welcome home.

We're a grace-driven community where the broken find healing, the weary find rest, and the lost find purpose. Here, we've witnessed marriages restored, dreams reignited, and lives completely transformed by the power of God's Word and Spirit.

No matter what you're facing or where you've been, you belong here. This is more than a church. It's family.

Scent of Water Ministry is a divine mandate received from the Lord Jesus Christ, with a message of restoration and hope to the downtrodden, downcast and weary. Every dry bone can resuscitate through the scent (fragrance, aroma) of water: God's Word and His Spirit. We teach and focus only on the Grace of God.
