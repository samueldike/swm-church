ABOUT US  
Scent of Water Ministry is a divine mandate received from the Lord Jesus Christ with a message of restoration and hope to the down trodden, down cast and weary. Job 14v7-9.  
We believe every dead dream can live again; every dry bone can resuscitate through the scent (fragrance, aroma) of water (God’s Word and His Spirit). We teach and focus only on the Grace of God.  

VISION  
Repositioning men for God’s very best.

MEET OUR SENIOR PASTOR
PASTOR SAMUEL INYENEABASI is the founder and Senior Pastor of Scent of Water Ministry. He is a dynamic teacher with a depth in the word of God that is easily applicable, with a dimension of the prophetic and words of wisdom. 
He is saddled with the mandate of Repositioning men for God's best. Over the years, this mandate has impacted individuals, families, marriages, youths, CEOs, campuses and corporate organizations.
Through his annual program "Singles Point Blank", he equips single people with enough practical scriptural knowledge to embrace their God's ordained relationship and dominate in marriage. 
Through his triweekly prayer program "Built to Last Marriage Prayer Network (BLM)", he is saddled with the burden of restoring order in marriages by interceding for marriages across the nations of the earth with countless testimonies following.
He is the author of the "Marriage Institution and Finance" and the highly sought after "Scent Daily Devotional".
Pst. Samuel is also the host of the radio podcast, Doxa Connect, which airs on radio station across Nigeria.
Through his ministry, many have encountered God, discovered God’s purpose for their lives and are excelling even in leadership.
Through the weekly Corpers Transition Network (CTN), he equips youth corpers with practical leadership skills to dominate the marketplace. Over the years, CTN has birthed countless high-flying CEOs.
Pastor Samuel is the founder and President of The Alpha Team (TAT), an interdenominational conclave where he dish premium knowledge to CEOs, Ministers, principals and leaders of empires who are engaged in running with God's heart-beat.
A much sought-after conference speaker, Pastor Samuel is known for stirring revival and prayer fire, step by step teaching on kingdom principles, and practical ways of replicating success.
He has a mandate to reposition men for God's best, and sees inherent potentials in people.
He is married to Pastor Mary. They are blessed with five children, and they currently reside in the city of Abakaliki, Ebonyi state.
